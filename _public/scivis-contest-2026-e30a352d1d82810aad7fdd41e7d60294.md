## About the Contest

The IEEE SciVis Contest is an annual event that challenges participants to apply state-of-the-art visualization techniques to real-world scientific datasets. The 2026 contest was organized in Boston alongside the IEEE VIS Conference. It featured new datasets and tasks designed to push the boundaries of scientific visualization and data analysis.
**The best submission was awarded a cash prize of $1000.**

## Using the NSDF-OpenVISUS Cookbook

Participants were encouraged to use the NSDF-OpenVISUS Cookbook as a practical guide for:

- **Data Access:** Learn how to efficiently access and manage large scientific datasets using hierarchical Z-order storage and OpenVISUS tools.
- **Visualization Workflows:** Explore example workflows for atmospheric and ocean datasets, including multi-resolution visualization and interactive analysis.
- **Testing and Validation:** Follow best practices for testing your visualization approaches and validating results using the provided templates and utilities.

The cookbook provided step-by-step instructions, code snippets, and explanations to help participants get started quickly and make the most of the contest datasets.

## How Participants Got Started

1. Reviewed the contest datasets and requirements on the [official website](https://sciviscontest2026.github.io/).
2. Used the NSDF-OpenVISUS Cookbook to learn about data access, visualization, and analysis techniques.
3. Adapted the example workflows and templates to their own contest entries.
4. Tested their results and shared their findings with the SciVis community.
